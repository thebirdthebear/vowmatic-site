A Highlight Film, Drafted For You

Vowmatic scans all your footage for the two-to-five-second peak moments, weaves them into a full highlight film — getting ready through the dance floor — and hands you a review board where you drag, swap, and pin shots before export. You direct; it does the digging.

Multicam Edits

A multicam edit of your ceremony, toasts, and dances — picking the best camera at every moment, tracking speakers, reactions, and emotion. You fine-tune instead of starting from scratch.

B-Roll, Scored and Ranked

Every category of b-roll — details, portraits, prep, dancing — gets scored and ranked, with near-duplicates and dead moments filtered out. Your best alternates are one click away.

Best Moments Highlighted

The best clips get marked as favorites so you find them fast. Blurry, shaky, or unusable clips get flagged so you can skip them entirely.

Every Clip Sorted by Event

AI labels each clip — ceremony, toasts, first look, portraits, getting ready. Browse by category instead of scrolling hundreds of files.

Story Builder

Listens to your audio, finds the emotional highlights, and helps you pick the best moments. Exports a timeline right into your editor.
