Questions

How long does the sale last?

25% off every plan through December 31. Monthly subscribers get the sale price on every payment through Dec 31, then renew at standard price starting January. Annual subscribers lock in the sale rate for the full year, since it's a single upfront payment. Credits bought during the sale keep that price forever, since they never expire.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. Your first wedding includes everything — multicam edits, clip sorting, ratings, story builder, the works. No limits, no watermarks, no credit card.

What if I need more weddings than my plan allows?

Extra weddings are $19 each on Pro and Studio plans. Or upgrade to the next tier anytime.

Does my footage get uploaded anywhere?

No. Your video never leaves your Mac. Only small frame thumbnails are sent to the AI for clip sorting — your full video and audio files stay on your machine.

Which editing software works with it?

Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve. The output is built specifically for each editor so everything just works when you import it.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime and keep access through the end of your billing period. Credits never expire.

Do credits expire?

Never. Buy a credit today, use it six months from now.

What cameras does it work with?

Anything that shoots MP4 or MOV. Tested with Sony, Canon, DJI drones, and GoPro. Audio works with MixPre, Zoom, and standard WAV files.

How long does it take?

About 45 minutes total. You'll spend about 5 minutes reviewing clips, then it runs on its own for the rest.

Do you have a referral program?

Yes. After your first purchase, you'll get a personal referral code. Share it with a friend — they get an extra free wedding, and you get one too.
